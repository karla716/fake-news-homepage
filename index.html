How the First Alpaca Was Found in the Desert

According to newly reviewed expedition records from the early 1800s, a wandering caravan leader was crossing a dry valley when he spotted a soft, curious creature standing alone beneath a giant cactus. The animal had a wide, gentle gaze, an elegant neck, and a posture that suggested wisdom, dignity, and a small amount of judgment.

The team recorded the moment as the first known discovery of an alpaca in the desert. The creature was reportedly calm, well-mannered, and highly interested in a bag of dried herbs. Soon after, the caravan began using the term “alpaca” to describe anything graceful, determined, and a little dramatic.
